How it works. Every question carries a sensitivity from 1 to 5 and a level from icebreaker to intimate. You start on safe ground. After each one you tap deeper or lighter and the deck moves exactly one step — never further. Getting past sensitivity 3 asks you to confirm the other person is up for it, which with somebody you met an hour ago is the whole point.

On a phone: swipe the card right to go deeper, left to go lighter, up to skip. On a keyboard: ← → to change level, space for the next question, S to save — press ? for the rest.

What the labels mean

On the card
Warm-up How personal the question is, from Icebreaker up to Intimate. The deck only ever moves one step at a time.
2 · Light Sensitivity, 1 to 5. How badly it could land with someone who wasn't expecting it. Anything above 3 needs you both to agree first.
Food, cooking & dining The topic. You can switch topics off in Decks → Mute topics.
freq 86 How common this question is in real conversation, scored 0 to 100. A high score means you will genuinely hear it, so it is worth learning first.
Prompt An invitation, not a question. "Tell me about…" rather than something with a yes-or-no answer.
asked 3× You have had this one before, and how many times.
What is your …? The grammar pattern the question is built on, at the bottom of the card. Useful if you are drilling a shape rather than a topic.
The level gauge
5 rungs Every deck covers a range of levels. Lit rungs are ones you have reached; the ones marked not in this deck need a different deck to get to.
12 more How many questions are left at that level that you have not seen yet.
